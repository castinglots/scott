# Spirit Quest: Way of the Saints

### Discover the Saints. Explore the World. Grow in Faith.

**Every saint has a story. Every journey is an adventure.**

**Spirit Quest: Way of the Saints** is a Catholic adventure game for iPhone that brings inspiring lives from Church history into everyday outdoor exploration. Follow the illustrated **Adventure Atlas**, discover nearby saint encounters, and explore their stories using your iPhone camera and augmented reality.

## Your adventure begins here

### Explore the world around you
Turn walks, neighborhood outings, and trips to the park into opportunities to learn about the saints. See nearby saint markers on the **Adventure Atlas** GPS map. As you approach a saint marker, it turns blue to show that an encounter is ready.

### Find saints through the camera
Follow the prompts on your screen and look around through the camera to locate the hidden saint. Tap the saint portrait to make a discovery.

### Discover the stories of the saints
Get to know **225 Catholic saints** through colorful, collectible illustrations and **four historical fact cards for each saint**. Complete all four story chapters to unlock a biography scroll about that saint's life.

### Discover Daily Grace
Use **Grace Near Me** to explore **25 daily saint encounters** in a nearby search area. Return for fresh opportunities to discover saints and collect Grace.

### Explore parks and quests
Discover parks, follow special quests, and experience Catholic history while spending time outside.

### Earn spiritual rewards
As you discover saints, collect Grace, progress through pilgrim ranks, and unlock illustrated **Saintly Treasures**. Your progress reflects the discoveries you've made along the way.

### Make it your adventure
Choose a saint illustration for your profile, set your own username, and track your saint collection, ranks, and rewards.

## A journey of faith and discovery

Whether you're a parent exploring with your children, a student learning the history of the Church, or someone seeking inspiration from the lives of the saints, **Spirit Quest: Way of the Saints** invites you to discover their faith, courage, and compassion.

**Explore. Discover. Be inspired.**

*Spirit Quest: Way of the Saints is being prepared for release on the Apple App Store.*
