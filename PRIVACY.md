# Spirit Quest — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 10, 2026

Spirit Quest (bundle ID `com.scottaitken.spiritquest`) is an iPhone augmented-reality game. This policy describes how information is accessed and used in the app.

## Location

With your permission, Spirit Quest uses your device's location while the app is in use to display nearby Spirits, measure proximity to encounters, and support park quests and other real-world discovery features. You can turn off location access in iOS Settings, but location-dependent features may stop working.

For live maps and park searches, the app contacts mapping and geographic-information services. Requests may reveal the map area or park-search area being viewed and technical network data such as the device's IP address to the service providers.

## Camera and augmented reality

With your permission, Spirit Quest uses your device's camera to display augmented-reality encounters. The reviewed app does not contain a feature that uploads camera photos or videos to a Spirit Quest account. You can revoke camera permission in iOS Settings.

## Game progress

Collected Spirits, seed totals, rewards, settings, and other game progress are stored locally on your device. The reviewed app does not provide a Spirit Quest account or developer-operated cloud sync for this information. Uninstalling the app may remove locally saved data, subject to the device's backup settings.

## Maps and external providers

The app uses external mapping or park-information services, including OpenFreeMap resources, OpenStreetMap Overpass API endpoints, and Apple mapping or geocoding services. These providers may receive your IP address, requested map regions, geographic search terms, and related technical request information. Their processing and retention practices are governed by their own policies. Spirit Quest does not control these providers' records.

## Ads, analytics, and tracking

The reviewed build does not implement a developer-operated account system, in-app advertising, or a developer-operated behavioral analytics service. This does not mean that external map providers or operating-system services receive no technical information. If the app's functionality or providers change, we will update this policy and any applicable App Store privacy disclosures.

## Data retention and your choices

You can change camera and location permissions in iOS Settings. Locally saved game progress generally remains on the device until removed through supported in-app controls, app deletion, or iOS device management; backup copies may persist according to your device settings. For information retained by external mapping providers, consult those providers' policies.

## Children and safe play

Spirit Quest is a location-based outdoor game. Parents and guardians should supervise children appropriately. Never enter unsafe areas or private property while playing.

## Updates

We may update this privacy policy when features, service providers, or practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

## Contact

For questions about Spirit Quest privacy, use the [project support issue page](https://github.com/castinglots/scott/issues/new). **GitHub issues are public; please do not include sensitive or personal information in them.** You may also contact the developer using the support contact provided on the app's App Store listing.
