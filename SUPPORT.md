# Spirit Quest — Support

Welcome to the official support page for **Spirit Quest**, an outdoor augmented-reality game for iPhone.

## Get help or report a problem

**[Open a Spirit Quest support request](https://github.com/castinglots/scott/issues/new)**

Support requests are handled through GitHub Issues. You may need a free GitHub account to post a request.

**Privacy reminder:** GitHub Issues are public. Do **not** post your precise location, home address, phone number, email address, Apple Account details, or screenshots containing personal information. Please include only the general problem and steps to reproduce it.

You can also [view previously reported problems](https://github.com/castinglots/scott/issues).

## Common questions

### The map isn't showing nearby Spirits

Make sure Location Services are enabled on your iPhone and that Spirit Quest has permission to access location while the app is in use. Try moving to an open outdoor area with a clear GPS signal and an active internet connection.

### The AR camera isn't opening

Open **Settings** on your iPhone, find **Spirit Quest**, and allow **Camera** access. Spirit Quest's AR mode requires a compatible iPhone with ARKit support.

### I can't collect a Spirit

Make sure you're close enough to the encounter, then follow the in-app camera directions and tap the Spirit when prompted. GPS accuracy can vary, particularly indoors or near tall buildings.

### The map or parks won't load

Verify that your internet connection is working. Map tiles, geographic search, and some park information rely on external services.

### What happens to my collection if I remove the app?

Your Spirit collection and progress are primarily saved on your device. Deleting or reinstalling the app may erase locally stored progress. Different app bundle identifiers can also create separate app installations.

### Where is the privacy policy?

Read the [Spirit Quest Privacy Policy](https://github.com/castinglots/scott/blob/master/PRIVACY.md).

## Reporting an issue

Please include:
1. The feature affected (Explore, AR, Find and Seed, Album, Rewards, or Parks).
2. A short description of what happened.
3. The iPhone model and iOS version, if comfortable sharing.
4. The app version shown in your build or App Store listing.
5. The steps that reproduce the problem.

Please avoid sharing precise GPS coordinates or other personal information.
