# Privacy Policy — Spirit Quest: Way of the Saints

**Effective date: November 21, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Spirit Quest: Way of the Saints** (the “App”) uses information when you play. The App is a Catholic educational exploration game for iPhone featuring GPS-based saint encounters, an illustrated map, camera-based augmented reality, and collectible saint stories.

**Questions about privacy?** Email **[sacredheartstudio@gmail.com](mailto:sacredheartstudio@gmail.com)**.

## 1. Information used by the App

**Location.** With your permission, the App accesses your location while you use location-based features. This enables nearby saint markers, distance-based encounters, Grace Near Me, park searches, and quests. The App may use your location and nearby map areas to request mapping or place information from external providers. You may turn location access off in iOS Settings, but location-based features will be limited.

**Camera and augmented reality.** With your permission, the App uses your camera and ARKit to display and locate illustrated saints in an augmented-reality view. The reviewed version of the App does not have a feature that uploads camera photos or videos to an account operated by the developer. Camera access can be disabled in iOS Settings; this prevents camera-based encounters.

**Profile, preferences, and game progress.** The App allows you to enter an explorer username, select a saint portrait, choose sound settings, and collect saint story cards and rewards. This information, along with collected Grace, completed encounters, discovery dates, ranks, and progress records, is stored locally on your device. The reviewed version does not require account registration and does not offer developer-operated cloud synchronization for this game progress.

**Support messages.** If you email the support address or submit an issue through GitHub, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your email address, message, and any diagnostic details you provide. We use this information to answer requests, investigate problems, and improve support. Emails and support requests may be handled by the relevant email or GitHub provider under its own policies. GitHub issues may be publicly visible, so do not post sensitive information there.

## 2. External map and place-information services

The App uses external mapping and geographic-information services to display the Adventure Atlas and to look up parks and places. These include **MapLibre**, **OpenFreeMap**, **OpenStreetMap/Overpass**, and **Apple Maps/MapKit** components or services.

When the App requests map tiles, park data, geographic searches, or other network resources, the service providers may receive technical connection details, such as your IP address, the map area or coordinates involved in the request, search terms, and device/network request information. Their handling and retention of this information are governed by their own privacy practices. The App developer does not control those providers' systems.

## 3. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

Based on the reviewed App source for version 1.3.60, the App does **not** include a developer-operated login service, advertising network, behavioral analytics SDK, or advertising-tracking feature. We do not sell players' personal information.

This does not mean that third-party map providers, Apple's platform services, or the providers used for support receive no technical information; see the sections above.

## 4. Why information is used

Information is used to:
- Show nearby virtual saint encounters and manage distance-based discoveries;
- Run the camera-based augmented-reality experience;
- Save your chosen username, portrait, preferences, discoveries, and unlocked rewards on the device;
- Find and display maps, parks, and geographic information;
- Answer your support questions and troubleshoot problems.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

Locally saved game information remains on your device until it is cleared by an available in-app control, by removing the App and its local data, or through relevant iOS device-management or backup processes. Removing the App may permanently erase your saved progress. Device backups may retain a copy according to your backup settings.

For support messages that you send, you may request deletion or assistance by emailing **sacredheartstudio@gmail.com**. We will handle requests relating to information within our control, subject to applicable obligations. External service providers may maintain records under their own retention policies.

## 6. Your choices

You can:
- Change location and camera permissions in your iPhone's **Settings**;
- Choose or change your explorer username and saint portrait in the App;
- Toggle the sound preference;
- Stop using the App or delete its local data by uninstalling it, subject to device backups;
- Contact us with questions about personal information you supplied directly.

## 7. Children and outdoor safety

The App is intended to be family-friendly. Parents and guardians should supervise children using location-based experiences. Never enter roads, private property, closed or unsafe areas to reach a virtual saint marker.

The App does not require players to submit a real name, date of birth, or email address to create an in-app explorer profile. Support communications are optional; parents or guardians should assist children who need support. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children through a developer-operated account system. If you believe personal information was supplied to our support channels by a child, contact us.

## 8. Security and changes

We aim to minimize the information handled directly by the developer. No internet-connected service is guaranteed completely secure, and third-party mapping and communication providers operate their own systems.

We may update this policy as App features, integrations, or data practices change. Any update will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
