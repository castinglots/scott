## Frequently asked questions

### How do I find saints near me?

Open **Explore** and allow location access when prompted. The **Adventure Atlas** map shows nearby saint markers. Move closer to a marker; when it turns blue, select it to begin searching.

### How do I find a saint through my camera?

Allow camera access when prompted. Follow the prompts at the bottom of the camera screen and move your phone slowly to locate the hidden saint. Tap the saint portrait to collect a story card.

### What is Grace Near Me?

**Grace Near Me** lets you discover 25 daily saint encounters in a nearby search area. Move closer to each saint marker to begin searching.

### What do I collect?

Each saint has four collectible historical fact cards. Discovering all four chapters unlocks an extended saint biography scroll. Discoveries also contribute to your Grace progress, ranks, and spiritual rewards.

### Why aren't map markers appearing?

Check that Location Services are enabled for the app and that you have a working internet connection. GPS accuracy can vary, especially indoors or near tall buildings. Try an open outdoor area.

### Why doesn't the AR camera open?

Check iPhone **Settings** and make sure camera permission is allowed for Spirit Quest: Way of the Saints. Augmented-reality features require a compatible device.

### Can I change my username or saint portrait?

Yes. Open **My Profile** to edit your username or select a different saint portrait.

### What happens if I uninstall the app?

Progress is saved on your device. Removing the app may remove locally saved data, so avoid uninstalling unless necessary.

## How to report a problem

When emailing [sacredheartstudio@gmail.com](mailto:sacredheartstudio@gmail.com) or [submitting a support request](https://github.com/castinglots/scott/issues/new), include the feature affected (Explore, Grace Near Me, Saints in the Park, My Saints, Saintly Treasures, or Profile), a short description of the issue, the app version, and the steps to reproduce it. You may include your general iPhone model and iOS version, but never share private information.
